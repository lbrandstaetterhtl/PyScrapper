Program flows

The two paths through the OCR, step by step. Click a step — or use prev/next — to see what happens there and where it can fail. The tabs switch between flows.

How to read these

The spine on the left is the happy path in execution order. Dashed boxes are decision points, red ones are error exits; the buttons under „Where it can go from here“ jump to them.

img_to_text is what happens inside the Rust module for one call. Bandcamp captcha is the surrounding Python side: when the OCR is called and what is done with its answer.
