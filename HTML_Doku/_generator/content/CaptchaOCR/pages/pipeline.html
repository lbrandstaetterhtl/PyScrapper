CaptchaOCR / pipeline

From image to letters

Everything before the network runs in load_and_split(). Its job is to turn one captcha into an ordered list of 28×28 tensors, one per letter. Whether the OCR succeeds is decided here more often than in the network: a letter that is lost or split here cannot be recovered later.

The steps

StepFunctionWhat happens
1load_image Decodes the file with the image crate. Any format it knows works; Bandcamp delivers JPEG.
2grayscale → to_rgb8 The picture is reduced to gray and then widened back to three equal channels, because the next step works on RGB.
3background_color The median of every channel over all pixels. Background is assumed to cover more than half of the image, so the median is its colour.
4make_black_and_white Each pixel's Euclidean distance to that background colour, scaled to 0–255. Above 32 it becomes white (ink), otherwise black.
5median_filter(1, 1) A 3×3 median removes salt-and-pepper noise left by the JPEG and by the captcha's speckles.
6image_split_up connected_components with 8-connectivity labels every white blob; get_bounding_boxes collects a BBox with pixel count per label, sorted left to right.
7filters A blob survives if it has at least 30 pixels, fills at least 15 % of its box, and is at least 40 % as tall as the tallest blob. That drops dots, lines and stray noise.
8crop_letter Cuts the box out of the binary image with 3 pixels of padding, clamped to the image edges.
9to_emnist Scales the crop so its longer side is 20 pixels (triangle filter) and centres it on a black 28×28 canvas — the layout of the EMNIST dataset.
10Tensor::from_vec Pixels divided by 255, shaped (1, 1, 28, 28): batch, channel, height, width.

What the segmentation assumes

The approach only works for captchas whose letters do not touch. Two letters that share a pixel after binarising become one blob, one crop and one prediction — the result is then missing a character and none of the candidates can be right. A letter that breaks apart (a thin i, a broken stroke) can either vanish under the size filters or turn into two predictions.

The order of the letters is the order of the boxes' left edges. Overlapping boxes from slanted letters are therefore still read left to right, but a short letter tucked under a long one's overhang can swap places.

An image without letters returns one empty string

If every blob is filtered out, load_and_split returns an empty list, the beam search starts from ("", 0.0) and never extends it, and img_to_text returns [""] rather than raising. The Bandcamp browser then submits an empty answer.
