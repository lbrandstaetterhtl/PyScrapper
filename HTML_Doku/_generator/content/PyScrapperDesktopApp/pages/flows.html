Program flows

Every path the app takes, step by step. Click a step — or use prev/next — to see what happens there, what it can fail on and where it can go from here. The tabs switch between flows.

How to read these

The spine on the left is the happy path in execution order. Dashed boxes are decision points, red ones are error exits. The buttons under „Where it can go from here“ jump to the step they name — that is how the branches and failure paths are reachable, since they are not part of the linear sequence.

Startup is the spine of the whole app and the other flows hang off it: Login and Launcher are two of its steps expanded, Loading data is what runs just before the main window, and Exit and save is what happens when it closes.
