Program flows

The engine as a sequence of steps rather than a call graph. Click a step — or use prev/next — to see what happens there and where it can go from here. The tabs switch between flows.

How to read these

The spine on the left is the happy path in execution order. Dashed boxes are decision points, red ones are failure exits. The buttons under „Where it can go from here“ jump to the step they name — that is how branches, retries and error paths are reachable, since they are not part of the linear sequence.

Start with Link to byte stream: it is the whole engine end to end, and the other four zoom into one of its steps.
